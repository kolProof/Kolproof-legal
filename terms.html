KOLProof Terms of Service

Version 2026-10-27 · Operated by KOLProof

1. The service

KOLProof is a Telegram-based marketplace where Web3 projects and KOLs find each other and agree campaign deals. As features are released, KOLProof also provides smart-contract escrow, milestone review and dispute handling. Deals are agreements between the project and the KOL; KOLProof is not a party to the campaign itself.

2. Eligibility and accounts

You must be at least 18 and able to enter a binding agreement. Your account is tied to your Telegram user ID. You are responsible for activity on it and for keeping your Telegram account secure. Provide accurate information and at least two genuine social profile links of your own.

3. Verification

“Verified” next to a social link means KOLProof checked that the profile showed your verification code. It does not verify your identity, your audience size, or the quality of your work. Follower counts and engagement figures you enter are self-reported unless stated otherwise. KOLProof does not currently perform identity verification.

4. Platform fee

KOLProof charges a platform fee of 3% of the agreed KOL payment. The project owner pays the fee in addition to the KOL payment (example at 3%: a 1,000 payment plus a 30 fee means 1,030 must be funded; the KOL receives 1,000 and KOLProof receives 30).

  • The fee, the KOL payment and the total to fund are shown to both parties before a deal is accepted, and the rate is locked into the deal at that moment.
  • A fee is earned only when the related milestone payment is released. Fees for milestones that are cancelled or refunded are returned to the project. If a dispute splits a milestone, the fee is earned in proportion to the amount awarded to the KOL.
  • The fee is a KOLProof platform charge. It is not a tax. Any taxes that apply to you are separate and your responsibility.

5. Escrow, delivery and disputes

Where escrow is available, funds are held by a smart contract, not in KOLProof’s database. Either party may open a dispute under the terms agreed in the deal. Settlements follow the contract’s rules; a timer expiring alone does not release disputed funds unless the agreed deal terms say so. Blockchain transactions are final and cannot be reversed by KOLProof.

6. Prohibited conduct

Do not impersonate others, submit fake or borrowed profiles, fabricate evidence, run scams or pump-and-dump schemes, share seed phrases or private keys, attempt to bypass platform rules or fees, or harass other users.

7. Account restrictions
