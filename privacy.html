KOLProof Privacy Policy

Version 2026-10-27 · Applies to the KOLProof Telegram bots

1. Who we are and what this covers

KOLProof is a Telegram-based marketplace that connects Web3 projects with influencers and Key Opinion Leaders (KOLs). It lets projects find KOLs, agree campaign terms, and (as those features are released) hold funds in escrow, review deliverables and resolve disputes.

This policy explains what personal data we collect when you use the KOLProof bots, why, who can see it, how long we keep it and what rights you have. KOLProof is currently operated by KOLProof, Abuja, Federal Capital Territory, Nigeria (the “data controller”). Registration status with the Nigeria Data Protection Commission: not_registered.

Contact for privacy matters: kolproof@gmail.com, or use /privacy in the bot.

Identity documents: KOLProof does not currently verify identity or collect ID documents. If we introduce identity verification, we will use a specialised provider, ask for your separate consent, collect only what is needed, and update this policy before doing so.

3. Legal basis (Nigeria Data Protection Act, 2023)

We process personal data on these bases: consent (you accept this policy and the Terms before your profile is created; you may withdraw it, which may limit what we can offer); performance of a contract (operating your account and your deals); legal obligation (record-keeping, responding to lawful requests); and our legitimate interests in preventing fraud and securing the platform, balanced against your rights. If you are in another jurisdiction (for example the EEA or UK), the data-protection law that applies to you may give you additional rights.

4. Who can see your information

  • Other users: your public profile (name, KOLProof username, bio, picture, role, social links and their verification status) may be shown to other users when marketplace features are enabled. We do not show private wallet details or sensitive data on public profiles.
  • Your deal counterparty: deal terms, deliverables, evidence and relevant wallet addresses.
  • KOLProof administrators: a small number of authorised people, with access limited by role. Administrator notifications contain only what is needed to triage (for example display name, role, counts and IDs) and never include biographies, link contents, identity numbers, secrets or keys. Admin actions are logged.
  • Blockchain networks: transactions are published on public blockchains (for example Solana and Ethereum) and are visible to everyone.
  • Service providers that help us run KOLProof (see section 5), under appropriate confidentiality and data-protection terms.
  • Authorities, where required by law or to protect rights and safety.

We do not sell your personal data.

5. Third-party services

  • Telegram delivers all bot messages. Telegram processes your data under its own privacy policy.
  • Hosting and database: Github.
  • Blockchain RPC and node providers: to read and verify on-chain activity, only when blockchain features are enabled. These providers receive public blockchain data and standard connection metadata.
  • Identity-verification provider: none at present.
  • Social platforms: we store the links you give us. We do not currently connect to your social accounts through OAuth.

Some providers (including Telegram) may process data outside Nigeria. Where we transfer personal data abroad we rely on the safeguards permitted by the NDPA.

6. Security

We use access controls limited by role, encrypted connections, audit logs of administrative actions, restricted storage of secrets, and rate limiting. No system is perfectly secure and we cannot guarantee absolute security or complete anonymity. Blockchain activity is public by design.

8. Your rights and how to use them

You may ask to access your data, correct it, delete it, restrict or object to certain processing, receive a portable copy, or withdraw consent. You can do this from the bot with /privacy, or email kolproof@gmail.com. We will respond without undue delay and within the time required by law. We may need to verify that you control the Telegram account making the request. Deletion is refused while you have active deals and does not remove records we must keep by law or data written to a public blockchain. You also have the right to complain to the Nigeria Data Protection Commission.

9. Children

KOLProof is for adults only (18 or older). If you believe a minor has registered, contact us and we will act on it.

10. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a material way we will publish a new version and the bot will ask you to review and accept it before you continue. Opening this page does not count as acceptance; only tapping “I accept” in the bot does.
